# Variance commentary draft, FY2025 vs FY2024

_Source: rule-based fallback. Number check PASSED: every number traces to the facts. Controller sign-off required._

**Headline.** Net sales grew 2.3% to EUR 517.5 m, but EBITDA fell EUR 17.6 m to EUR 48.0 m (margin 13.0% to 9.3%).

**Main drivers (EUR m).**
- Fibre cost: -7.8
- Volume: +6.5
- FX (SEK): -4.8
- Price: -4.5
- Energy cost: -2.3

**Market vs controllable.** Fibre, energy prices and the weaker SEK explain EUR 15.0 m of the decline together. The controllable part sits in pricing: fixed annual contracts moved -3.4% (EUR -9.0 m) while indexed contracts moved +1.1%.
29 accounts are EBITDA-negative after cost-to-serve (EUR -5.1 m).

**Recommended actions.** Index clauses at contract renewal; order and delivery rules for loss-making accounts; fix the energy drift at Sweden Central, flagged by the weekly rule in the week of 2025-05-19 (EUR 0.45 m excess in the year).

**Open questions.** Renewal calendar of fixed contracts: to be confirmed. Root cause of the energy drift: to be confirmed by maintenance.
